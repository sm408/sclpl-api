# HANDOFF

**`docs/cli-rebuild/HANDOFF.md`** — where the work stands, and what to pick up next. Not
in this vault; this note exists so links resolve.

## What it holds that this vault does not

- The exact commit for each milestone
- "Things to know before the next milestone" — the sharp edges someone picking the work
  up would otherwise find by hitting them
- What is deliberately not done, and why that is a decision rather than an omission

## Division of labour

| | |
|---|---|
| [[SPEC]] | What to build. Normative |
| **HANDOFF** | Where we are, and what is next |
| **This vault** | How it works and why it is shaped this way |
| [[Decision Log]] | What was decided, and what it replaced |

HANDOFF answers "what do I do now". This vault answers "why is it like this". They point
at each other rather than repeating each other.
